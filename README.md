## How I work

I try to get something playable early and change it based on what testers actually do, not what I assumed they'd do. I also care about the lore and context around a mechanic, since that's usually what makes players care about it.

## Before games

I spent 14 years as a supervisor at Stormtech, mostly on the systems side. I worked on process improvement, brought in technology to take repetitive work off people’s plates, and wrote the SOPs the team worked from. The part I was best at was tracking a problem back to its root cause and fixing it there, so it stopped coming up, instead of having everyone learn to work around it.

That’s how I approach game systems too. When something feels off in a mechanic, I want to know why before I start patching it.
